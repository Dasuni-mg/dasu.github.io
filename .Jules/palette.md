## 2026-05-19 - [Skip to Content Link]
**Learning:** Adding a 'Skip to Content' link is a low-effort, high-impact accessibility improvement that significantly benefits keyboard-only users by allowing them to bypass repetitive navigation. It requires a semantic <main> target with tabindex="-1" to ensure focus is correctly moved across all browsers.
**Action:** Always include a skip link and .sr-only utility in projects with top-level navigation.
