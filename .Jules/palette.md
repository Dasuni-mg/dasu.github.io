## 2026-06-12 - [Skip to Content Implementation]
**Learning:** For keyboard and screen reader users, a skip-to-content link is essential for navigating past repetitive header content. Wrapping the primary content in a semantic <main> element with a targetable ID and tabindex="-1" ensures a smooth transition and focus management across all browsers.
**Action:** Always include a skip-to-content link and a semantic <main> landmark when improving the accessibility of a single-page or navigation-heavy website.
